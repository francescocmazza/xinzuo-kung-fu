# The Gongfu of Xinzuo

## A practical book about what really makes a kitchen knife perform.

A kitchen knife may look simple, yet what happens on the cutting board is shaped by steel, heat treatment, geometry, sharpening and technique working together.

*The Gongfu of Xinzuo* began as a practical training tool for people working with Xinzuo knives every day. From that starting point it grew into a guide for cooks, professionals and curious readers who want to understand not only which knife to choose, but why different knives behave differently in the hand and on the board.

From metallurgy and blade construction to grip, cutting technique, maintenance and sharpening, the book connects theory to what can actually be observed, felt and practised in real use.

**Gongfu** is skill built through time, study, repetition and mindful practice. This book is an invitation to develop it.
