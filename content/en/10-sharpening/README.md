# Sharpening section

This section contains the practical sharpening material for the book. Keep the chapters procedural, observable and linear: introduce the physical goal first, explain the tool or concept before the step that depends on it, and present each action in the order in which the reader performs it.

Follow the manuscript-wide sentence rule in `EDITORIAL_REQUIREMENTS.md`: complete the main action or proposition before adding comparisons, qualifications or exceptions whenever accuracy allows. The sharpening chapters should feel like a clear guided process rather than a sequence of nested caveats.
