# The Gongfu of Xinzuo

*A Practical Guide to Kitchen Knives*<br>
*Materials, Shapes and Techniques for Masterful Cutting*

A kitchen knife looks simple: a handle, a blade and a very thin edge. In use, it can chop, glide, rock, steer, wedge, separate or crush. What the cook feels comes from the interaction between the knife, the ingredient and the cutting movement.

This book explains those interactions and builds practical skill in choosing, using and caring for kitchen knives. Technical concepts appear when they explain something that can be seen or felt in use, so knowledge can grow into judgement and skill.

Xinzuo products provide concrete examples from real design, manufacturing and use. The goal is to understand why differences between knives matter in the hand and on the cutting board, not to memorize product names or specifications.

> **The central idea:** steel creates possibilities, heat treatment develops them, geometry turns them into cutting behaviour, sharpening restores the edge and technique determines what the cook experiences.

The Preface explains the meaning of *gongfu* in the title, the purpose of the book and the background behind it.
