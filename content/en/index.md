# The Gongfu of Xinzuo

*A Practical Guide to Kitchen Knives*<br>
*Materials, Shapes and Techniques for Masterful Cutting*

A kitchen knife looks simple: a handle, a blade and a very thin edge. In the hand, however, it can chop, glide, rock, steer, wedge, separate or crush. What the cook experiences comes from the interaction between the knife, the ingredient and the movement used to cut it.

This book is a practical foundation for understanding those interactions and developing greater competence in the choice, use and care of kitchen knives. Technical concepts are introduced when they help explain something that can be observed or felt in real use, so that knowledge can progressively become judgement and skill.

Xinzuo products are used throughout the book as concrete examples drawn from real design, manufacturing and use. The aim is not to memorize product names or specifications, but to understand the principles that make the differences between knives meaningful in the hand and on the cutting board.

> **The central idea:** steel creates possibilities, heat treatment develops them, geometry turns them into cutting behaviour, sharpening restores the edge and technique determines what the cook experiences.

The meaning of *gongfu* in the title, the purpose of the book and the background from which it was developed are explained in the Preface.
