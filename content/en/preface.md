# Preface

The title of this book begins with an idea that is much broader than martial arts. *Gongfu* can describe a skill acquired through time, study, repetition and disciplined practice: knowledge that has been understood, tested and gradually turned into reliable ability.

That is the spirit in which this book was conceived. A kitchen knife is an apparently simple object, but using one well requires more than learning a few movements. Competence grows when practice is supported by understanding: when the user can recognize why a knife behaves in a certain way, distinguish meaningful differences from superficial ones and connect what is felt in the hand with the choices behind the tool.

The purpose of this book is to provide a strong technical and practical foundation from which the reader can develop an advanced level of competence. It cannot replace years of professional experience, and mastery cannot be obtained by reading alone. Its role is to make practice more intelligent: to help the reader observe more carefully, understand established solutions, ask better questions and learn faster from experience. In that sense, *gongfu* begins by learning what others have already discovered, then practising until the underlying principles can be understood, judged, adapted and eventually improved.

The book grew from personal study and experience together with knowledge developed inside Xinzuo through design, manufacturing, testing, sales and daily contact with cooks and customers. The ideas presented here are therefore a synthesis of experience and study rather than an immutable final truth. Xinzuo provides many of the real examples, but the subject of the book is the kitchen knife itself.

Knowledge improves when it is shared. For that reason the book is also available free of charge on GitHub at **https://github.com/francescocmazza/xinzuo-kung-fu**. The repository is particularly useful for collaborative revision: corrections, comments and proposals for improvement are welcome there or by email at **francescoclaudiomazza@gmail.com**.

This book is a starting point from which knowledge can become practice, practice can become experience, and experience can gradually become *gongfu*.
