# Blade construction

This section explains how blade construction changes the role of the materials around and at the cutting edge.

Keep the distinction between core-and-cladding Damascus, full pattern-welded Damascus and historical crucible-pattern steels explicit. Explain the physical construction first and then its practical consequence.

When a Xinzuo construction deliberately uses layering as a functional design variable—as in the customized 110-layer full-Damascus blades—state that design purpose clearly. Explain the benefit being pursued before the measurement boundary or qualification. Do not make a verified engineering choice sound merely decorative by leading with what it cannot guarantee.

Follow the sentence-level linearity rules in `EDITORIAL_REQUIREMENTS.md`: present the construction or mechanism before the comparison, qualification or limitation whenever that order remains accurate.
