# Foundations

This section establishes the mental models used throughout the rest of the book.

Keep the explanations practical and readable before adding technical depth. A reader should understand the main relationship first and then the qualification or exception.

Apply the manuscript-wide linear sentence rule in `EDITORIAL_REQUIREMENTS.md`: avoid front-loaded comparisons, delayed objects and caveats inserted inside the core subject-action-proposition path. Comparisons and limitations should normally follow the proposition they qualify.
