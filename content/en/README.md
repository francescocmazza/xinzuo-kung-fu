# English source

`content/en/` is the source of truth for the multilingual publication pipeline.

Make editorial changes here first. Translations should preserve the meaning, structure and natural reading order of the English source without copying awkward English syntax.

The manuscript should read as a continuous practical book, not a collection of product notes. Follow `EDITORIAL_REQUIREMENTS.md` and `docs/editorial-principles.md`, especially the rules on narrative progression, plain-language compression, sentence-level linearity, terminology, content preservation and translation.

At equal meaning, always prefer the clearest, most direct and shortest natural formulation. Keep technical complexity only where accuracy, understanding or safety requires it.

**First-use terminology rule:** review terminology in the actual publication order. The first time the manuscript uses a word or concept that a general reader may not know, explain it immediately in plain language. A later specialist chapter may deepen the explanation, but the reader must never need a future chapter to understand the sentence in front of them. If the technical term is unnecessary, use the familiar word instead.

**Repetition rule:** teach each subject fully once, in the chapter where it belongs. When another chapter needs the same idea, repeat only the part required for that new explanation or give a short reminder. Do not reproduce the same mini-lesson in several places.
