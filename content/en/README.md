# English source

The English manuscript under `content/en/` is the source of truth for the multilingual publication pipeline.

Editorial changes should be made here first. Active translations are refreshed from this source by the repository's translation workflow and should preserve the meaning, structure and natural reading order of the English text without mechanically copying awkward sentence construction.

The manuscript is intended to read as a continuous practical book rather than as a collection of isolated product notes. Follow `EDITORIAL_REQUIREMENTS.md` and `docs/editorial-principles.md` when adding or revising material, especially the rules on narrative progression, sentence-level linearity, terminology, content preservation and translation.
