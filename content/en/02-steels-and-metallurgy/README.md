# Steels, metallurgy and handle materials

This section explains material choices as interacting systems rather than as prestige rankings or isolated specification lists.

Introduce the practical effect first, then explain the mechanism one level deeper. Keep composition, manufacturing route, heat treatment, geometry and use connected in the order in which the reader needs them.

Apply the manuscript-wide linear sentence rule in `EDITORIAL_REQUIREMENTS.md`: complete the main proposition before inserting a comparison, limitation or caveat whenever accuracy allows. Material descriptions should say what a steel, wood or composite is and does before comparing it with another material.

For handle materials in particular, avoid repeated catalogue-style templates such as “its advantage is” and “its compromise is”. Explain the material in natural prose, including what it is made from, how it is produced or sourced, its useful characteristics, its limitations and its care requirements.
