# Knife-type section

This section teaches readers and staff to choose and explain knife forms from the work they perform rather than from names or appearance alone.

Keep profile explanations discursive and practical: describe the geometry, the food and task, the movement it encourages, and the limitation. Use the exact Xinzuo model only as a verified example of the form.

All knife-profile images must follow the requirements in `EDITORIAL_REQUIREMENTS.md`, including full uncropped visibility and a cutting edge that reads downward. The verified curved-paring image is the Xinzuo B9H-SG asset stored as `../assets/images/approved/knife-shapes/paring-curved.jpg`.

Apply the manuscript-wide linear sentence rule throughout this section: state what the form does first, then add the comparison or trade-off where it can be understood without interrupting the core sentence.
