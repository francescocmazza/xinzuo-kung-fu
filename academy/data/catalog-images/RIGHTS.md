# Product photograph rights

These product photographs are extracted from the Xinzuo/Hezhen catalogue and V15 price list supplied by Francesco Claudio Mazza for use in Xinzuo Academy staff training. Rights in the source photographs remain with their respective owners; the repository's text licence does not grant independent rights to these photographs. The extraction does not generate or alter the depicted knife design.

Images use neutral IDs to avoid exposing the series or SKU in the quiz image URL. Source mappings, PDF crops and workbook row numbers are recorded in the backend catalogue manifest.

The 2026-10-19 resolution update also includes photographs supplied through the owner's Dropbox Marketing/Immagini folders and the xinzuo.it storefront. Rights remain with the original owners. Source identities and image dimensions are recorded in `academy-backend/data/image-sources.json`. These are native source images, not AI reconstructions or artificial enlargements.
